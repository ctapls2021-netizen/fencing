The brand's call-to-action button — every "Request Free On-Site Estimate", "Schedule Your Free Consultation", "Call Now".
```jsx
<Button variant="primary">Request Free On-Site Estimate</Button>
<Button variant="outline-gold" icon="phone">Or Call Now: (800) 562-5770</Button>
<Button variant="accent" fullWidth>Schedule Your Free Consultation</Button>
```
- primary (blue) for main action on white or photo; accent (gold) is reserved for the single conversion action per view (form submit, ad CTA).
- outline-gold pairs with primary on dark photos; white is the primary on blue panels.
- `tracked` gives the spaced-out uppercase label from the brand guide.
