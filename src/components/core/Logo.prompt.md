The Mulholland Brand logo — use in headers, footers, ad frames. Never redraw it.
```jsx
<Logo variant="primary" height={34} />
<Logo variant="white" height={40} />   // on blue
```
- primary: blue road-sign with "OUT OF THE ORDINARY" (marketing default, works on white and photos).
- white / white-outline: "ALUMINUM TECHNOLOGIES" lockup, for blue or navy grounds only.
- mark / mark-white: circular road mark for avatars, favicons, small badges.
- Requires `window.MB_ASSET_BASE` (default "assets/").
